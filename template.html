THIS MONTH’S HEADLINE

Building momentum, one improvement at a time.

From engine cooling to telemetry and chassis development, our team has been preparing the car for its next round of testing.

Here’s a look at the work happening across Brum Eco Racing. This example uses the articles from Esther’s draft, with a refreshed layout and shorter, easier-to-scan copy.

ENGINEERING SPOTLIGHT

Keeping the engine cool under pressure

The ICE team has introduced a preliminary fan-based cooling system to draw hot air away from the engine fins during longer runs. They have also adjusted the baseplate to improve clearance around the fuel tank and lines, and installed a new Hall sensor with a Deutsch connector to support more reliable acceleration.

ELECTRICAL

Telemetry: from planning to integration

The electrical team has been preparing its telemetry system while awaiting components. Testing plans are ready, and with most parts now delivered, the next step is implementing the subsystems and beginning integration.

CHASSIS

Machining the details that matter

The chassis team has been preparing the body and machining revised rear-wheel and brake-caliper mounts. The new brakes have also been bled ahead of ground testing.

About Us

Brum Eco Racing is a fully student-led hypermileage racing team at the University of Birmingham. Since 2016, we have been designing, building and racing ultra-efficient vehicles in the Shell Eco-marathon, pushing the boundaries of energy efficiency through engineering innovation and teamwork.
