FEATURED STORY / SHELL ECO-MARATHON 2026

Seven Years in the Making: Our Return to the Track

In June, Brum Eco Racing travelled to Silesia Ring in Poland for Shell Eco-marathon Europe 2026, marking our first appearance on track in seven years. Our vehicle comfortably passed technical inspection, clearing the way for two competitive on-track sessions. After months of preparation, seeing the car running on the circuit was a major milestone and a testament to the work put in by the team. Beyond the track sessions themselves, the competition provided an opportunity to put our engineering, telemetry and race operations to the test in a real competitive environment. The experience has given us a strong foundation to build upon as we prepare for 2027. There's plenty more to tell from Poland, and we'll be sharing the full story on our new website, launching soon.

CHASSIS

Starting from Scratch: A New Chassis for 2027

Our chassis department is taking on one of the team's biggest projects this season: designing and developing an entirely new chassis. With a focus on reducing weight, improving efficiency and creating a more robust platform, the project will lay the foundations for the next generation of Brum Eco Racing vehicles.

POWERTRAIN DEVELOPMENT

Exploring New Directions in Propulsion

Development is underway on two fronts this season. Our bespoke battery-electric motor driver, designed by the team last year, has entered testing, marking an important step towards developing our own electric powertrain. Meanwhile, our internal combustion department is investigating the possibility of moving to a smaller engine. The team is exploring how a different engine platform could improve efficiency without comprimising on performance.

About Brum Eco Racing

Brum Eco Racing is a fully student-led hypermileage racing team at the University of Birmingham. Since 2016, we have been designing, building and racing ultra-efficient vehicles in the Shell Eco-marathon, pushing the boundaries of energy efficiency through engineering innovation and teamwork.
