FEATURED STORY / SHELL ECO-MARATHON 2026

Seven Years in the Making: Our Return to the Track

In June, Brum Eco Racing travelled to Silesia Ring in Poland for Shell Eco-marathon Europe 2026, marking our first return to the track in seven years. After months of preparation, our internal combustion vehicle made it onto the circuit for two race attempts. While mechanical issues ultimately prevented us from completing a valid run, getting back onto the track was a significant achievement for the team. The competition gave us invaluable experience in vehicle development, testing and race operations, alongside real-world performance data that we simply couldn't replicate in the workshop. We returned to Birmingham with a clearer understanding of our vehicle's strengths and weaknesses, and plenty of lessons to carry into the new season. Now, our focus is on turning that experience into a more reliable and competitive car for 2027.

CHASSIS

Starting from Scratch: A New Chassis for 2027

Our chassis department is taking on one of the team's biggest projects this season: designing and developing an entirely new chassis. With a focus on reducing weight, improving efficiency and creating a more robust platform, the project will lay the foundations for the next generation of Brum Eco Racing vehicles.

POWERTRAIN DEVELOPMENT

Two Powertrains, One Goal: Greater Efficiency

This season, we're expanding our ambitions with the development of both a new battery-electric powertrain and an improved internal combustion powertrain. While our electric project opens up new opportunities in motor control and battery systems, our ICE development will focus on addressing the reliability issues encountered in Poland and improving fuel efficiency.

About Brum Eco Racing

Brum Eco Racing is a fully student-led hypermileage racing team at the University of Birmingham. Since 2016, we have been designing, building and racing ultra-efficient vehicles in the Shell Eco-marathon, pushing the boundaries of energy efficiency through engineering innovation and teamwork.
